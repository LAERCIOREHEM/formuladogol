## Statistical contract
Documented individual counts: https://www.api-football.com/news/post/how-to-get-started-with-api-football-the-complete-beginners-guide (fixtures/players). Field names and meaning are documented, but historical semantics and availability are not fully certified. E04 independently checks raw/normalized agreement, integer bounds, positive regulation-minute proxy, explicit zero vs null, duel numerator/denominator and role-specific coverage. Fit gate: >=100 observations and >=70% coverage by rows, exposure minutes and >=45-minute appearances in every prior training season; row coverage drift <=20 percentage points. Bounds and cutoffs are predeclared research choices, not tuned on holdouts. Missing fields never filled with zero. Pass accuracy, xG, goals conceded and external rating excluded. Sparse goals/assists may be excluded by gate. Saves, passes, duel ratio etc are workload/involvement proxies, not proof of quality or causal offense/defense.
Per90 counts log1p transformed, training-only 1/99% clipping, opponent pre-match Elo regression with ridge penalty, standardized residual clipped to +/-3. Elo uses previously played fixtures after simulated seven-day delay, K=20, home +55; no current score used for current covariate. Opponent adjustment associative, not causal. Duel ratio has fixed Beta(2,2) smoothing, missing stays null. Recency half-life 180 days, 450 effective-minute prior at role mean, role-specific fixed weights. Rating 100/(1+exp(-0.8*z)), neutral 50. Separate offense/defense axes; missing axis null, partial overall flagged, confidence reduced. Confidence is heuristic exposure*field coverage*axis availability, not a calibrated probability. Percentile by position, ties midrank. No verified ESPN mapping or current roster claim.
